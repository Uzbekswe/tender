# Answer key for the demo samples

All names, tax IDs (STIR), addresses, dates and amounts are invented. The PDFs are
generated by `backend/demo/make_samples.py` (run it from `backend/` with
`.venv/bin/python demo/make_samples.py`). This file lists what the generator plants,
so a check on these files can be compared with the expected result.

- Deadline 30.12.2026, 10:00. Start price 186 000 000 sum with VAT. Scoring: maximum 10, pass mark 7.
- Requirements the bids are checked against:
  - RAM at least 16 GB (section 2, row 2); SSD at least 512 GB; display 15.6"; battery 45 Wh and 8 h.
  - Warranty at least 24 months (section 2, row 9); delivery within 30 days.
  - Tax certificate dated no earlier than 30.11.2026 (30 days before the deadline).
  - Licence or dealer letter valid on the submission date.
  - At least 3 similar contracts in 2024 and 2025, with acceptance acts.
  - Form 1 guarantee letter (appendix 1); offer validity at least 90 days.
- **Planted contradiction:** the warranty is 24 months in section 2, row 9, but 12 months in
  section 6.2 (contract terms). Expected: the clarification letter asks which applies.
